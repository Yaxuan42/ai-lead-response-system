# Parallel Routes

Run two routes at the same time, with separate pages and sales lists.

## Route A: 餐饮 / 茶饮招商加盟

- Branch: `codex/franchise-food-beverage`
- Demo: `https://ai-franchise-leads.vercel.app`
- Offer: `¥6,800` pilot, `¥15,000` growth setup, `¥1,500/month` support.
- Best prospects: chain restaurant, tea, coffee, bakery, snack, and quick-service brands that are actively recruiting franchisees.
- Success test: can one extra high-intent franchise candidate justify the pilot fee?

## Route B: AI 成交响应台

- Branch: `codex/open-market-revenue-desk`
- Demo: `https://ai-revenue-desk.vercel.app`
- Offer: `¥8,800` pilot, `¥28,000` revenue desk setup, `¥3,000/month` support.
- Best prospects: any business with high deal value, existing inquiries, slow first response, and a clear follow-up owner.

## Weekly Split

- 60% effort: Route A until it gets the first paid pilot or 100 qualified outreach attempts.
- 40% effort: Route B to search for faster cash signals across other markets.
- Keep lists separate.
- Compare only outcome metrics: reply rate, calls booked, pilot close rate, and speed to payment.
